What changed in Version 1.3
  • Cleaner mobile-first interface and report workflow.
  • PDF and CSV downloads, pre-addressed email preparation, and optional device sharing.
  • Past Reports simplified to personal reimbursement status only.
  • Clear History preserves the user's name and profile settings.
  • Mileage rate is fixed in the app instead of editable.
  • Merchant names are no longer evaluated as vague business purposes.
